The history of engineering

The Prometheans bring together historians, engineers and scholars in science and technology studies whose work takes engineering practice as its subject. We meet in two places: in sessions at the annual meeting of the Society for the History of Technology, and in a monthly working group hosted by the Consortium for History of Science, Technology and Medicine.

  • SHOT annual conferences Panels, workshops and roundtables proposed to the programme committee each year. Seven sessions in 2026; four panels in Luxembourg in 2025.
  • Engineering Studies working group A monthly hour for work in progress, book discussions and lightning talks. Eighteen sessions since October 2023.

Convened by Ellan F. Spero (Massachusetts Institute of Technology) and Ryan Hearty (Johns Hopkins University).
