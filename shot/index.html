SHOT annual conferences

Each year the group circulates a call for papers connecting the meeting’s themes to engineering history, builds the panels in the working group over the winter and spring, and proposes them to the programme committee of the Society for the History of Technology.

  • 2026 Virtual Annual Meeting 22–24 October · seven sessions, eighteen papers
  • 2025 Luxembourg 9–11 October · four panels, fourteen presentations
  • 2023 Los Angeles — being added.

Convened by Ellan F. Spero (Massachusetts Institute of Technology) and Ryan Hearty (Johns Hopkins University).
